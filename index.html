No Microplastics. No Mess.

T-Squeeze is the sleek, reusable tea infuser that filters out harmful microplastics and brews cleaner, stronger tea—without the waste or cleanup.

You’ve been drinking microplastics with your tea — and no one told you.
A 2019 study found that a single plastic-based tea bag can release over 11 billion microplastic particles into your cup.

Zero Microplastics

Filters hidden plastics out of your tea. Just pure leaves and full flavor.

Reusable & Eco-Safe

Designed to eliminate single-use waste. One T-Squeeze can replace hundreds of tea bags.

Bold, Clean Brew

Engineered for maximum flavor extraction with zero mess. Rinse and reuse instantly.

About T-Squeeze

Our mission is to revolutionize your tea ritual with cleaner brews, reducing waste and protecting your health.

T-Squeeze was designed to address the hidden dangers of microplastics in your tea, ensuring that every cup is pure. Our innovative design eliminates single-use waste while maintaining the full flavor of your favorite tea.

Driven by sustainability and cutting-edge engineering, we are committed to a cleaner and smarter future with every brew.
